No fabric photo on your phone? Download a sample and use it in the app.

  1. Tap Download under a photo. On iPhone or iPad you can also press and hold the photo and choose Save to Photos.
  2. Open FabVision AI and choose the photo from your library when the app asks for a fabric photo.

These photos are only for trying the app. Your own photos stay private to your account.
