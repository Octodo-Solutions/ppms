FabVision AI

Fabric to model photos

Upload a photo of your fabric, pick the garment and style, and get a catalog-ready model photo in a minute or two. No photographer, no studio, no model booking.

Get it on Google Play App Store: coming soon

Made for Indian textile sellers

For manufacturers, wholesalers, boutiques and resellers who need good product photos for every design and every colour, without a photoshoot for each one.

How it works

  1. Upload your fabric. The app checks the photo and reads the pattern, colour, texture and weave.
  2. Choose the garment and style. Saree, suit, kurti, lehenga, sherwani and more, with the model, pose, theme and background you want.
  3. Generate. Your model photo is usually ready in a minute or two.
  4. Save or share. Save it to your phone, or share it on WhatsApp or any other app.

What you can make

One design, many colours

Show 2 to 5 colours of the same design on different models in one photo.

The complete look

Add a dupatta, bottom, blouse or other matching fabric to the outfit.

Your choice of model

Create new AI faces, reuse your saved faces, or upload your own photo.

Short videos

Catalog posters

Put your photos on your own poster template and edit the text.

Everything in one place
