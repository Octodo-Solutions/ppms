Delete your FabVision AI account

You can delete your FabVision AI account at any time, either in the app or by email. FabVision AI is provided by OctoDo Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Option 1 — In the app (quickest)

  1. Open FabVision AI and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab, then tap Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Tick the confirmation box and tap Delete my account. Then tap Delete account to confirm.

Your account is closed immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — By email (no app needed)

Send an email to octodosolutions682@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Delete my FabVision account
  • The phone number or email address you sign in with. For Sign in with Apple, this is the email Apple shows for FabVision, which may be a privaterelay.appleid.com address.

Email a deletion request

We close the account within 7 days, after we verify that the request comes from the phone number or email address registered to the account. To verify, we may ask you to reply from that email address, or to confirm a message we send to that phone number or email. We confirm by email when the account is closed.

What happens next

Your account is closed now; all your photos and data are permanently erased after 30 days. Payment records are kept as required by law, without your personal details.

  • Immediately: your account is closed and you can no longer sign in. Unused credits are lost.
  • For 30 days: nothing else is removed, so a mistaken deletion can be undone. Email us within 30 days and we can reopen the account.
  • After 30 days, automatically and permanently, we erase:
    • your fabric photos, face photos and generated images and videos;
    • your profile and business details;
    • your feedback;
    • your sign-in record.
    After this the account cannot be reopened.
  • What is kept: payment and invoice records (order, amount, date) as required by applicable law. They are kept with your name, phone number and email removed, and are used for nothing else. Analytics events are kept as described in our Privacy Policy (section 6).

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Fabric photos: open the Gallery tab, then Fabrics. Open the fabric, tap the bin icon at the top and tap Delete. You can also long-press the fabric.
  • Generated images and videos: open the Gallery tab, then Generations. Open the item, tap the bin icon at the top and tap Delete. You can also long-press the item.
  • Face photos: open the Gallery tab, then My Faces. Tap the bin icon on a face (or long-press it) and tap Delete. To remove all the face photos you uploaded at once: open the Profile tab, tap Settings, and under Your data tap Delete my face data, then Delete uploaded faces. This removes them from your account. AI-generated faces stay until you delete them in My Faces.
  • Profile and business details: open the Profile tab, tap the ⋮ menu, then Edit Profile or Edit Business Profile.
  • Anything else: email octodosolutions682@gmail.com and say what you want deleted. We acknowledge your request within 24 hours and resolve it within 15 days.
