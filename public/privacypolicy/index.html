1. Information we collect

1.1 Account and profile

  • Sign-in details. These depend on how you sign in:
    • Phone: your mobile number, verified with a one-time password through Firebase Authentication. On Android the app may suggest your phone number using Google's phone-number hint, and only if you choose to use it.
    • Google: your Google email address and name.
    • Sign in with Apple: a stable Apple identifier, and your email (which can be an Apple private-relay address) and name if you choose to share them. We also keep an encrypted Apple sign-in token, so that we can tell Apple to revoke FabVision's access when you delete your account.
  • Profile and business details you enter: display name, business name, business type, city, the garment types you work with, and optionally your GSTIN, WhatsApp number, a contact email or a contact phone number.
  • Account records: your account ID, sign-in method, the platform you signed up on (Android or iOS), your sign-up date, your credit balance and credit history, and referral information (your referral code, who referred you, and referral rewards).
  • IP address at sign-up. We record the IP address you sign up from and use it to detect abuse of free and referral credits (fraud prevention). We keep it for 12 months (section 6). From the next app/server update it is no longer sent to our billing system.
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), so that we can tell you when your images or videos are ready.

1.2 Content you upload and create

  • Fabric photos, which we store as the original and as processed copies, plus the original file name and file size.
  • Face photos that you choose to upload as a model reference. Before your first face upload we ask you to confirm that the photo is of you, or that you have that person's permission.
  • AI-generated content: catalogue images, model photoshoots, colour variations, AI faces and short marketing videos, together with the settings and any notes or edit instructions you typed.
  • Feedback you send in the app (a star rating and an optional message), and an optional reason if you delete your account.

1.3 Purchases

  • Android (Razorpay): order and payment IDs, the amount, the number of credits bought and the payment status. Razorpay collects your card, UPI or bank details directly in its own checkout. We never see or store them. We pre-fill Razorpay's checkout with your name, phone and email.
  • Android checkout diagnostics: when a payment fails, technical facts about your phone's payment set-up (Android version, WebView version, Google Play services status, whether UPI apps are installed). These help us fix checkout problems. They do not include payment details.
  • iPhone and iPad (Apple In-App Purchase): Apple transaction IDs, the product bought, the price, currency and storefront, and refund notifications from Apple. Apple handles payment. If Apple asks us about a refund request, we send Apple information about how you used the credits (consumption information) only if you agreed to this when you bought credits.

1.4 Usage, device and diagnostics

  • App analytics: which screens you open and which features you use (for example "generation started" or "purchase completed"). These events are tied to your FabVision account ID. We send them to PostHog and to Google Analytics for Firebase (see section 4). We restrict them to a fixed list of fields that never includes your phone number, email address, photos or the text you type. We do not use advertising identifiers (no Android Advertising ID, no Apple IDFA), and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects a technical report (the error, device model, operating-system version and app version). Crash reports are not linked to your account.
  • Install referral (Android): if you installed FabVision through a friend's invite link, Google Play tells the app the referral code from that link.
  • Server logs: request logs (time, path, response status, IP address) used for security and to fix problems. We keep them only for a limited time.

1.5 Device permissions

  • Camera: to photograph fabrics and, if you choose, a face reference.
  • Photos: to pick images you choose, and to save generated images and videos to your gallery.
  • Notifications: to tell you when results are ready.

FabVision does not access your contacts, your precise location or your microphone.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account, and to sign you in.
  • To generate your images and videos. This includes classifying fabrics, detecting colours and checking that a face photo is suitable.
  • To process purchases, add credits, handle refunds and chargebacks, and keep the accounting and tax records the law requires.
  • To run referral and welcome credits, and to prevent abuse of them (for example with your sign-up IP address and phone number).
  • To send notifications you can turn off in your phone's settings.
  • To answer support requests, feedback and grievances.
  • To understand how FabVision is used and to fix errors, through analytics and crash reports.
  • To meet legal obligations and to protect FabVision and its users.

We do not use the face photos you upload to train AI models. Since 19 October 2026 we do not use the fabric photos you upload to train AI models. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. AI processing

To produce your results, your fabric photos, any face photo you use, previous results you choose to edit, and your notes are sent to our AI providers:

  • Google (Gemini API), which processes your content to create your results under Google's Gemini API terms.
  • Kie.ai, which runs AI image and video models on our behalf. For images, Kie receives a time-limited link (valid for 2 hours) to the photo in our storage. For videos, the image is uploaded to Kie's file service (kieai.redpandaai.co).

We send your content to these providers only so they can create your results. They process it under their own terms, on servers that may be outside India. Kie.ai states that it deletes files uploaded to it after 24 hours, generated media after 14 days and request logs after 2 months (Kie.ai API documentation, "Data Retention Policy", checked 19 October 2026).

AI results can vary and may not exactly match your fabric. Please check results before using them commercially.

5. Storage and security

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Photos and videos are kept in private cloud storage (Amazon Web Services), encrypted at rest with AES-256 (Amazon S3 encrypts every new object by default). The app gets short-lived signed links to view them; they are not public.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore and iOS Keychain).
  • Access to admin tools is restricted to authorised OctoDo staff.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

7. Deleting your account

In the app (Android and iOS):

  1. Open the Profile tab, then tap Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Read what happens, optionally tell us why you are leaving, tick the confirmation box, and tap Delete my account. Then tap Delete account to confirm.

What happens:

Your account is closed now; all your photos and data are permanently erased after 30 days. Payment records are kept as required by law, without your personal details.

In detail:

  • Immediately: your account is closed, you are signed out and you cannot sign in to it again. We revoke FabVision's Sign in with Apple access if you used it. Unused credits are lost.
  • For 30 days: nothing else is removed, so that a mistaken deletion can be undone. Contact us within 30 days and we can reopen your account.
  • After 30 days, automatically: we permanently erase your fabric photos, face photos, generated images and videos, your profile and business details, your feedback and your sign-in record (Firebase Authentication). Analytics events are kept as described in section 6. Payment and credit records are kept as required by applicable law, with your name, phone number and email removed. After this the account cannot be reopened.

Without the app: follow the steps at octodo-solutions.github.io/ppms/deleteaccount/, or email octodosolutions682@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my FabVision account" and the phone number or email you signed in with. We close the account within 7 days, after we verify that the request comes from the phone number or email address registered to the account. The same 30-day timeline applies from when we close the account.
